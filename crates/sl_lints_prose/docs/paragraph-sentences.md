## Why

A paragraph of many sentences is a wall the reader skims. A readable paragraph holds one thought in two to five sentences: a claim, its reason, its limit. The default allows five, so a reasoned thought stays in one paragraph; a stricter project sets `max-sentences` lower. More facts than one thought become a list.

Paragraphs are runs of text lines; each list item counts as its own paragraph. Sentences are split as in `sentence-words`.

## How to fix

Keep the result and what the reader must decide or do. Turn the rest into a bulleted or numbered list, or cut it.
