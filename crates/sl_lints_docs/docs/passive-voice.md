## Why

A passive sentence hides who acts. "The file is read" leaves the reader to ask by whom, and when; "the engine reads the file" answers it. Passive sentences are also longer. A few are fine, where the actor does not matter; a page full of them reads as a list of things that happen on their own.

The share is an estimate. A sentence counts as passive when a form of "to be" comes before a past participle, with at most two words such as "not" or an adverb between them: "is read", "are not written", "was usually checked". The heuristic also counts an adjective such as "is tired". Pages with fewer sentences than `min-sentences` are not judged, and only pages in English.

## How to fix

Name who acts and make them the subject: "spl reads the manifest", not "the manifest is read". Keep the passive where the actor is unknown or does not matter.
