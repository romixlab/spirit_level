## Why

Code without prose leaves the reader to work out what it shows and why it is there. A page of listings one after another reads like a source file, and the reader stops at the first block they do not understand.

The lint adds up the lines of code blocks that follow each other with no prose between them. Headings, tables and figures between the blocks do not count as explanation.

## How to fix

Before each block, say in a sentence what it shows and what to notice. Cut a long listing to the lines that matter, and link the full file. Several variants of one example are clearer as content tabs with a sentence above them.
