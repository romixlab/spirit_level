## Why

A long page without sections gives the reader no map: they cannot see what it covers, skip what they know, or find a part again. Section headings also become the page's table of contents and its link anchors.

The lint counts the prose words of a page. Above `min-words`, the page needs at least one heading below its title (`##` or deeper).

## How to fix

Divide the page into `##` sections, each named after what it answers. A page that covers several unrelated topics is often better as several pages.
