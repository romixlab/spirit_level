## Why

Efton.sk gotcha 221 ("ADC may freeze around calibration or enable/disable, sequencing has to be observed carefully"), the G0/C0/WL paragraph:

> In 'G0 (and related 'C0 and 'WL families), there should be a 2 ADC kernel clock delay observed after disabling
> the ADC, and performing any other ADC-kernel-related operation (such as re-enabling ADC, or performing
> calibration, or writing the calibration factor).

The underlying cause (per the same page) is that the ADC is a dual-clock peripheral: the APB side the rest of the chip talks to and the kernel-clock side that actually runs the conversion need their signals resynchronized across the clock-domain boundary, and the timing of that resync depends on the exact ratio between the two clocks — which is why the symptom (an ADC that freezes) is hard to reproduce and depends on the specific clock configuration in use.

## Example

Typec_dongle's ADC calibration path (`0.20.7`) added exactly this wait after disabling the ADC and before the next kernel-related operation, closing a freeze that otherwise depended on the board's specific ADC clock divider to show up at all.

## How to fix

After disabling the G0/C0/WL ADC, wait at least 2 ADC kernel clock periods before re-enabling it, starting calibration, or writing the calibration factor register — compute the wait from the actual configured kernel clock, don't hardcode a microsecond figure that assumes one clock setting.

## How it is checked

Check: src mid · chip: G0 — needs the ADC kernel clock configuration to compute whether an observed delay between disable and the next operation is actually long enough.
